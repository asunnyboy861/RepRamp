Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

Information We Collect

RepRamp is local-first. We do not create accounts and we do not run analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

  • Workout data you enter (exercises, weight, reps, RPE, plans) is stored on your device.
  • Apple Health data (sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, body weight) is read only after you grant permission, and is used on-device to adjust recommendations. It is never uploaded.
  • Feedback you send (name, email, subject, message) is transmitted to our feedback endpoint so we can reply.
  • AI inputs (plan answers, screenshots or body photos) are sent only when you start an AI feature.

How We Use Your Information

  • To compute your training recommendations, statistics and rank progress.
  • To generate plans, muscle-balance analysis or plan imports when you request them.
  • To answer your support messages.
  • To verify purchases through Apple StoreKit.

AI Processing

AI requests are routed either through our proxy service to a third-party language-model provider, or directly to the provider whose API key you configured in Settings.

  • Photos are processed in memory, sent only when you tap Analyze, and discarded immediately afterward. RepRamp does not store your photos.
  • Prompts contain the workout context you chose to share, such as your goal and training days.
  • We do not use your inputs to train models, and we do not sell them.
  • If you use your own provider key, that provider's privacy policy also applies.

HealthKit

With your explicit permission, RepRamp reads sleep analysis, heart rate variability, resting heart rate and body mass through HealthKit, and writes completed strength workouts back to Apple Health. HealthKit data stays on your device and is never sent to our servers or to AI providers. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app.

Data Storage and Security

Training data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain and are never logged. You can export all of your data as CSV or JSON from Settings at any time. Deleting the app removes local data.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple — StoreKit purchases, HealthKit and App Store distribution.
  • AI providers — only for the requests you initiate, as described above.
  • Feedback backend — receives the support message you submit.

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Children

RepRamp is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your Choices

  • Deny HealthKit access — the app still works with your lifting history only.
  • Use the app without any cloud AI, or delete your AI provider key at any time.
  • Export or delete your data whenever you want.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "last updated" date.
